- Sharing an app.loreum.org link now shows a proper preview card with a title, a description, and the Loreum image. (#365)
- The app sitemap now lists all 18 docs pages. Before, it only had a handful of them. (#366)
- explorers.loreum.org now serves a real robots.txt and sitemap.xml. Before, those addresses returned the app page. (#367)
- Explorers preview cards now load their image from www.loreum.org, so they don't break on the apex redirect anymore. (#360)
