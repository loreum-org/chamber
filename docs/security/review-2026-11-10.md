# Chamber security review (2026-11-10)

Scope: `contracts/src` on main @ `4598366` (Chamber 1.3.0, the implementation behind the live mainnet Factory). Changes ship as Chamber 1.4.0.

## Summary

| ID | Severity | Title | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| R-1110-C1 | Critical | One-block board takeover drains every depositor | Fixed in 1.4.0 |
| R-1110-M1 | Medium | Board can spend any approval given to the chamber | Accepted risk (Chad, 2026-11-10) |
| R-1110-L1 | Low | A takeover is still possible if nobody responds within 2 days | Accepted design limit |
| R-1110-I1 | Info | Launch: seat at least 3 directors right after `createChamber` | Process |

Tools found nothing new. Slither 0.11 (all detectors): 0 High, 4 Medium, all in the known false-positive baseline. Aderyn: no High. All Foundry tests pass on 1.4.0 (forge 1.9), plus the Halmos suite.

## [R-1110-C1] One-block board takeover drains every depositor

**Location**: `BoardTypes.SEATING_DELAY`, `BoardLib.refreshSeating`, `Chamber._executeOne`

**Problem**: A new top seat could act after 1 block, and a spend executes as soon as quorum is met. Explorers NFTs are open mint and the vault asset is LORE, which directors may `transfer`. So anyone can borrow enough LORE to outweigh the third-ranked director, mint three NFTs, deposit and delegate to take 3 of 5 seats, and on the next block submit, confirm and execute `LORE.transfer(attacker, vaultBalance)`. The loan is repaid out of the stolen balance. Nobody has time to react.

**Proof**: `contracts/test/findings/Finding_R1110_OneBlockTakeover.t.sol`. On 1.3.0 the attacker borrows 300k LORE and takes 5.8M.

**Fix**:
- `SEATING_DELAY` is now **2 days**, measured in time (it was 1 block). A takeover has to hold its capital in public, where holders can out-delegate it or withdraw.
- A longer delay alone would let anyone freeze the board: bump a director out of the top seats and back in with a flash loan, and their clock restarts. So a seat that drops out keeps its clock, and only restarts if it is still out at a later block (`BoardStorage.droppedAt`, appended).
- Spends stay instant once a seat is mature. A spend delay was considered and rejected.
- `getSeatedAt` now returns a unix time. Chambers before 1.4.0 stored a block number there; the app and operator treat values under 1e9 as block numbers.

## [R-1110-M1] Board can spend any approval given to the chamber (accepted risk)

**Location**: `Chamber._validateTransaction`

1.3.0 let the board call only `transfer` and `approve` on the vault asset (R-1012-M3). That also meant the chamber could never use LORE's owner functions after the Safe hands LORE ownership over. Chad decided the board should have no restrictions on token calls, so 1.4.0 removes the check (and `WalletLib.requireNoThirdPartyPull`).

**Consequence**: a board majority can move LORE, or any other token, out of any wallet that approved the chamber, including tokens that were never deposited.

**Mitigation, not a restriction**: the app now approves only the amount being deposited, not unlimited. Holders who approved more by other means should lower the approval to zero after depositing. Self-calls and ProxyAdmin calls are still limited to pause/unpause and checked upgrades.

## [R-1110-L1] A patient, well-funded takeover still works

Seats go to delegated weight. Anyone with more capital than the third-ranked director can still take quorum and spend the vault, once they have held the seats in public for 2 days. Holders defend by delegating to honest directors or withdrawing during that window. Watch for new top seats (`getSeatedAt` in the future) and alert on them.

## [R-1110-I1] Launch order

Right after `createChamber`, the team should deposit and delegate to at least 3 Explorers it controls. Until 3 seats are filled, a single seated director can call `recoverSeats` and shrink the board to themselves.

## Not covered

Live mainnet behaviour of LORE and LoreumNFT (read from the handoff tests, not from chain). Economic cost of borrowing LORE. Halmos cannot deploy the proxy, so proxy paths are covered by Foundry tests only.
